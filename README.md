#HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee demographics, 
workforce trends, job satisfaction, and employee attrition.
The dashboard transforms HR data into interactive visualizations and key performance indicators that can help
organizations understand workforce patterns and identify areas that may require management attention.

## Business Objectives
The analysis focuses on answering key HR questions, including:
1. How many employees are currently in the workforce?
2. What is the employee attrition rate?
3. How does attrition vary across different departments and job roles?
4. Which demographic groups make up the workforce?
5. How satisfied are employees with their jobs?
6. What are the hiring and workforce trends?
7. Which factors may be associated with employee attrition?

## Tools & Technologies
**Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
**DAX** – Creation of calculated measures and KPIs
**Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
**Excel** – Initial data preparation and analysis

## Key Metrics
The dashboard includes key HR metrics such as:
- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate
- Average Employee Age
- Average Job Satisfaction
- Workforce and Hiring Trends

## Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard provides insights into:
### Employee Demographics
Analysis of the workforce by characteristics such as age, gender, and other employee attributes.
### Attrition Analysis
Analysis of employees who have left the organization, including attrition patterns across departments, job roles, and demographic groups.
### Job Satisfaction
Analysis of employee job satisfaction scores and their relationship with workforce outcomes.
### Hiring Trends
Visualization of employee hiring patterns over time to identify changes in workforce growth.

## Key Insights
The dashboard was designed to help HR teams:
- Identify areas with higher employee attrition.
- Understand workforce composition and demographic patterns.
- Monitor employee satisfaction.
- Track workforce and hiring trends.
- Use data-driven insights to support HR decision-making.
